From generated sheet to approval

Extract the inventory fields

The workflow reads the SKU, number of boxes or rolls, and units per box from the generated sheet.

Check the kit and catalog

Compare package counts against the physical kit. Check SKU existence, product status, and pack size against the catalog.

Resolve and revalidate

The operator confirms ambiguous products or applies a suggested correction. A sheet becomes ready for approval once all checks pass.

Six validation use cases

KIT-001

Approve a valid kit

Two boxes of gauze pads and one roll of tape match the sheet and catalog.

Outcome · Ready for operator approval.

KIT-002

Detect an unknown SKU

The sheet lists GASA-010, which is missing from the catalog.

Outcome · Approval waits for product confirmation.

KIT-003

Detect a pack size mismatch

The sheet lists 20 pads per box. The catalog lists 10.

Outcome · The review shows both values.

KIT-004

Detect a box count mismatch

The kit contains two boxes of 10. The sheet says one box of 20.

Outcome · Box count and pack size are flagged separately.

KIT-005

Detect a discontinued product

The SKU exists, but its catalog entry is discontinued.

Outcome · Approval is blocked until the product is resolved.

KIT-006

Revalidate a corrected sheet

The operator applies the suggested correction from 20 to 10 pads per box.

Outcome · The sheet becomes ready for approval when all checks pass.
